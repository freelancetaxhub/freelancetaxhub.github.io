Freelance Tax Calculator 2026 (USA)

This free calculator helps US freelancers, self-employed professionals, and 1099 contractors estimate their 2026 self-employment tax and federal income tax.

How Freelance Taxes Work in 2026

Freelancers must pay both federal income tax and self-employment tax. The self-employment tax rate is 15.3%, covering Social Security and Medicare. In addition, freelancers must pay federal income tax based on their tax bracket.
