How 1099 Taxes Work

Unlike W‑2 employees, 1099 contractors do not have taxes withheld. You must pay:

  • 15.3% self-employment tax
  • Federal income tax based on IRS brackets
  • Quarterly estimated payments

Who Receives 1099 Income?

  • Freelancers
  • Consultants
  • Gig workers
  • Real estate agents
  • Online sellers
  • Ride-share drivers

Tax Planning Tips for 1099 Contractors

  • Set aside 30% of income for taxes
  • Track all business expenses
  • Pay quarterly estimated taxes
  • Consider a SEP-IRA to reduce taxable income
